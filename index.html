What we do

Treatments offered

Comprehensive dermatology, cosmetology and trichology care, performed with the latest techniques right here in Tanuku.

Dermoscopy & Scanning

Computerised scanning of skin and hair for accurate, early diagnosis.

Chemical Peeling

Peels and fillers for the face to treat spots, scars and dullness.

Cautery for Warts & Moles

Painless removal of warts, moles and skin tags with cautery.

Hair Loss & PRP

Platelet-rich plasma therapy and GFC/IPRF growth-factor treatment for hair fall.

Laser Hair Removal

Laser and carbon-peel treatments for unwanted hair and skin brightening.

Hydrafacial & Botox

Deep-cleansing facials, anti-ageing, fillers and injectable skin boosters.
